Project plan

From research to a working NPU prototype.

This roadmap sets out the phases of the initiative in order. Durations are indicative and will be refined once Phase 1 is complete. Each phase ends with a review that decides whether the next phase starts.

In progress

Phase 1: Foundations

Understand the market and the current technology before designing anything.

  • 1a. Market research. Identify the pioneers in NPU and edge-AI hardware, what they build, the business information they have made public, and the expected growth of the market in space, automotive, healthcare and surveillance. Every figure is cited to its source, and the research is refreshed on a regular schedule.
  • 1b. State-of-the-art analysis of hardware design. Survey current accelerators and their architectures, including compute arrays, memory hierarchy and bandwidth, support for low-precision and sparse computation, power per unit of performance, and toolchain maturity. Output: a comparison and a list of gaps that our design can target.
Planned

Phase 2: Requirements and architecture

Turn the research into a specification and a first architecture.

  • Define target workloads for each sector and the performance, power, latency and precision each needs.
  • Identify sector constraints, such as radiation tolerance and temperature range for space, functional-safety requirements for automotive, and reliability and certification needs for healthcare and industry.
  • Choose the core architecture and list the intellectual-property (IP) blocks, such as the compute array, memory, interconnect and control logic.
Planned

Phase 3: Prototype

Build the design in hardware description code and test it on programmable hardware.

  • Implement the core IP blocks, with simulation and verification at each step.
  • Run a prototype on a field-programmable gate array (FPGA) board.
  • Benchmark reference models, such as image and sensor-processing networks, against the targets set in Phase 2.
Planned

Phase 4: Evaluation and product path

Measure the design against the state of the art and decide how to take it to market.

  • Compare the prototype with current accelerators on performance per watt, latency and cost.
  • Review the IP position and protect the core innovations.
  • Identify pilot partners in the target sectors and gather their feedback.
  • Plan a silicon run, for example through a shared multi-project wafer programme, with its cost and timeline.
Planned

Phase 5: Company and funding

Form the company and raise the funds needed to build the product.

  • Set up the legal entity, governance and founding team.
  • Prepare the pitch, financial plan and funding ask based on the results of Phases 1 to 4.
  • Raise funds and begin the first product development programme.

Throughout all phases, the community is kept informed through the website, our Discord community, and LinkedIn. Progress and spending are reported on the home page.
