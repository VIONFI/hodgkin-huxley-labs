Phase 1a: market research

Who builds NPUs and edge-AI accelerators, and where the market is going.

This page profiles the companies working on neural processing units (NPUs) and edge-AI chips, what they have made public, and how the market is expected to grow. Every figure is linked to its source and dated. Where a company has not published a number, we say so rather than estimate it.

Last updated: 7 November 2026. This page is refreshed on a regular schedule. Profiles marked "pending" are still being researched.

Market size

The market is forecast to grow fast, but estimates differ.

Two market reports published in 2026 give different starting points for the same market. Both expect strong growth, driven by automotive, robotics and on-device AI.

SourceBase year (2025)ForecastGrowth rate
Research and Markets, distributed by GlobeNewswire (27 Oct 2026) [1] USD 16.80 billion (2026 estimate: USD 20.60 billion) USD 106.00 billion by 2036 17.8% a year, 2026 to 2036
Global Market Insights [2] About USD 26.0 billion About USD 116.4 billion by 2035 About 16% a year, 2026 to 2035

Market size definitions differ between publishers, so the figures are not directly comparable. Both are shown here so you can judge for yourself.

Global Market Insights also reports the following for 2025 [2]:

  • NPUs are the largest category of processor, at about 30% of the market.
  • Inference, meaning running trained models, accounts for about 65% of AI processing.
  • Manufacturing is the largest end-use sector, at about 18%, with healthcare and transport also listed.
  • The five largest vendors held about 46% of the market between them: Apple (about 16.5%), Samsung (about 12%), NVIDIA (about 8.5%), Hikvision and Amazon.
Company profiles

Companies profiled so far.

Each profile lists what the company does and what it has made public. Figures are as reported, with the date and source.

IsraelEdge AI processors

Hailo

Edge AI processors for vehicles, consumer electronics and smart cameras

  • Flagship Hailo-8 processor, which runs machine-learning workloads that previously needed large servers. Also sells M.2 and Mini PCIe acceleration modules [3].
  • Raised a $136 million Series C in October 2021, led by Poalim Equity and Gil Agmon. Valuation was not officially disclosed; unnamed sources put it at about $1.13 billion [3].
  • Had over 100 clients in late 2021, having doubled its customer base in the last two fiscal quarters of that year [3].
  • In April 2026, a report citing regulatory filings said Hailo was planning a merger with a SPAC (a blank-cheque listing vehicle) to list in the US. This was not a confirmed announcement. The same report links it to a "need for liquidity", noting about 10% of staff were laid off in January 2026 and a $9 million loan was taken out [4].

Lesson for us: even well-funded edge-AI chip companies can face financing pressure. The company's growth and cash position matter as much as the technology.

EuropeEdge AI processors

Axelera AI

High-performance edge AI for defence, industry, robotics and security

  • Raised more than $250 million in a round led by Innovation Industries, with new investors BlackRock and SiteGround Capital. Total funding since 2021 is about $450 million [5].
  • The Europa AI processor was unveiled in October 2025. It reaches up to 629 TOPS at INT8 precision, a standard low-precision format for inference [5].
  • Developing a separate inference chiplet called Titania [5].
  • Says it has shipped to 500 customers across defence, industrial manufacturing, robotics and security [5].
Australia (ASX)Neuromorphic

BrainChip

Neuromorphic processors (Akida) for low-power edge AI, plus IP licensing

  • Half-year to 30 June 2026: revenue of US$1.22 million, up 19%. Net loss of US$12.02 million, wider than the US$9.36 million loss a year earlier. Operating expenses rose about 33% to US$13.65 million [6].
  • Cash position of US$20.3 million at 30 June 2026 [6].
  • Shipped its first production batch of 2,000 AKD1500 neuromorphic processors [6].
  • New IP licensing agreements with EDGEAI and ASICLAND could bring royalty revenue in future [6].
  • Next milestone: the AKD2500 chip is on track for a development milestone in late 2026 [6].
ChinaAutomotive AI

Horizon Robotics

AI chips and solutions for intelligent driving

  • 2025 revenue of RMB 3.76 billion, up 57.7% year on year. Gross margin was about 64.5% [7].
  • Products and solutions revenue rose 144.2% to RMB 1.62 billion, now 43% of total revenue [7].
  • Shipped over 4 million Journey-series automotive chips in 2025, up 39% [7].
  • Shipped 1.8 million mid- to high-level intelligent driving chips, nearly five times the previous year [7].
  • The company claims a 44.2% share of this segment in China's vehicles priced below RMB 200,000. This is the company's own claim, not an independent figure [7].
UKIP licensing

Arm

Processor IP, including the Ethos-U85 NPU, licensed to chip makers

  • Announced on 28 October 2025 an Armv9 edge-AI platform pairing the Cortex-A320 CPU with the Ethos-U85 NPU. The platform can run edge AI models with over 10 billion parameters [8].
  • Offered through its Flexible Access programme: customers get the IP, tools and resources upfront, at no or low cost, with reduced fees for qualified start-ups. Fees apply only to technology actually used in the final chip design [8].
  • Targets start-ups and large manufacturers of smart cameras, smart home, industrial automation and advanced interfaces [8].

Relevance for us: an NPU licensed as IP lowers the cost of entry for a small team. We will need to evaluate it against our own design.

Belgium (imec-backed)Space

EDGX

AI computers that run in orbit, so satellites need less ground processing

  • Raised a €2.3 million seed round, announced 11 August 2025, from imec.istart and the Flanders Future Tech Fund [9].
  • Its Sterna computer is built on NVIDIA hardware and runs AI workloads on the satellite. A companion software layer handles fault-tolerant operation and recovery from radiation effects [9].
  • Planned an in-orbit demonstration on a SpaceX Falcon 9 launch in February 2026. The source does not confirm whether it took place [9].
  • Has a €1.1 million contract with one unnamed satellite operator [9].

Early observations

What the research so far suggests.

These are observations from the profiles above, not conclusions. They will be tested as more companies are added.

  • Automotive is growing quickly. Horizon Robotics' revenue rose 57.7% in 2025 [7], and the market report names automotive as a main driver [1].
  • Funding and cash are tight for smaller companies. BrainChip has a loss-making half-year and US$20.3 million in cash [6]. Hailo's 2026 reports point to a need for liquidity [4].
  • Large, well-funded European players now exist. Axelera has raised about $450 million in total [5].
  • Licensing NPU designs is becoming easier. Arm's Flexible Access programme is aimed at start-ups [8], which lowers the barrier for teams like ours.
